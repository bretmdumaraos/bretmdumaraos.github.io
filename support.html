01 / Where to get help

Welcome to the support center for Bret Michael Dumaraos. This site brings independent app projects together in one convenient location.

For general project inquiries, you can find the developer on GitHub. An official support email has not been published on this site yet.

02 / Support for a specific app

Different apps may have different update schedules and support resources. Open the relevant project in the app gallery to see verified available links and check the app’s own documentation, user guide, or support options where published.

03 / Report a site issue

If you notice a broken link or a visual problem, refer to the project’s repository or the developer’s GitHub profile to find a suitable contact method. Please avoid posting passwords, tokens, health details, or other sensitive personal information publicly.

04 / Legal and privacy questions

For the portfolio site, see the Privacy Policy or Terms of Service. Questions about an individual app’s user data should be directed to that app’s own published policy or support channel.
