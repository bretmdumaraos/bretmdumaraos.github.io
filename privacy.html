Privacy policy.

A clear look at how this portfolio website works, what information it uses, and where to find privacy details for individual apps.

01 / What this policy covers

Effective date: October 19, 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how the publicly accessible Bret Michael Dumaraos portfolio website at bretmdumaraos.github.io operates. The website is an informational gallery for apps and digital projects by Bret Michael Dumaraos.

Scope note: This policy covers the portfolio website itself. It does not automatically describe or replace the data practices, Google Drive access, or authentication features of any individual mobile application shown here.

02 / Information and preferences

The current portfolio website does not have account registration, a contact form, advertising trackers, or its own analytics scripts. Search and category filtering run in your browser and are not transmitted to a portfolio-owned backend.

If you change the site theme, your browser may store the preference locally under bmd-theme so the site can display that preference on later visits. You can clear this using your browser’s site-data controls.

03 / Hosting and third-party links

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information such as IP address, browser details, and access logs as part of providing website hosting. For more details, please read GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Links to GitHub repositories, application websites, and app stores take you to external services governed by their own privacy practices. The portfolio is not responsible for those third-party websites.

04 / Individual app privacy policies

Each app may have different features and different data practices. An app that uses Google Sign-In, Google Drive, or other connected services needs an app-specific, accurate privacy policy that explains the information requested, its purpose, sharing, retention, deletion, and applicable Google API requirements.

Please use the relevant app’s own privacy policy from its project page when available. An app’s Google Drive or other API permissions should not be represented by this portfolio policy unless this policy is specifically updated to cover that app.

05 / Your choices

You can browse the site without signing in. You can avoid following external links and clear any saved local display preference through your browser settings. For questions about data handled by a particular app, consult that app’s own policy or support information.

06 / Policy updates

This page may change if the portfolio gains new features or integrates third-party tools. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

07 / Contact

For questions about the portfolio, see the support page or visit the developer’s GitHub profile.
